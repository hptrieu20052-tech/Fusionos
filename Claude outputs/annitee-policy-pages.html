Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Every AnniTee product is made to order — printed and crafted just for you after you place your order. Our items are produced at trusted fulfillment facilities in the United States and Vietnam, depending on the product type, and shipped directly to your door.

Processing Time

All orders require 2–4 business days of processing before shipment. During this time your item is printed, quality-checked, and packed. Orders placed on weekends or US holidays begin processing the next business day. During peak seasons (Black Friday, Christmas), processing may take 1–2 additional days.

Shipping Times

After processing, estimated delivery times are:

  • United States: 5–10 business days
  • Canada: 10–15 business days
  • United Kingdom: 10–15 business days
  • Germany: 10–15 business days
  • Australia: 10–15 business days
  • Rest of world: 10–20 business days

Total delivery time = processing time + shipping time. These are typical estimates, not guarantees — carriers can occasionally run late during holidays or severe weather.

Tracking

You will receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. Please allow 24–48 hours for tracking information to update after you receive it. Orders containing multiple items may ship in separate packages, each with its own tracking number, at no extra cost to you.

Customs & Import Fees (International Orders)

Orders shipped outside the United States may be subject to import duties or taxes determined by your local customs authority. These charges, if any, are the responsibility of the recipient.

Incorrect Address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we are happy to reship it — the buyer covers the reshipping cost. If you notice an address mistake, email us within 12 hours of ordering and we will correct it before the order ships.

Lost or Stuck Packages

If your tracking has not updated for 10+ business days or your order seems lost, contact us at support@annitee.com — we will investigate with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, send a free replacement or issue a full refund.

Questions? Email support@annitee.com — we reply within 24 hours, 7 days a week.

Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your satisfaction is our priority. Because every AnniTee item is printed on demand especially for you, our refund process is simple and doesn't require you to ship anything back.

Our 30-Day Guarantee

If your order arrives with any of the following issues, we will send a free replacement or issue a 100% refund — your choice, and no return needed:

  • Damaged in transit (torn, stained, broken)
  • Misprinted, blurry, or wrong design
  • Wrong item, wrong size, or wrong color compared to what you ordered
  • Significantly different from the product description or photos
  • Confirmed lost in transit by the carrier

To claim, email support@annitee.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue. We resolve most claims within 24 hours.

How Refunds Are Issued

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 3–5 business days of approval. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take an additional 5–10 business days for the amount to appear on your statement.

What Is Not Covered

  • Ordering the wrong size or changing your mind after the item has been produced — each piece is made uniquely to order, so please review the size chart on each product page carefully before purchasing. If you're unsure, email us first — we're happy to help you choose.
  • Damage caused by improper care (see care instructions on the product page).
  • Shipping delays by the carrier within the estimated windows in our Shipping Policy.

Even in these cases, reach out — we review every request individually and will always look for a fair solution.

Order Cancellation

You can cancel or edit your order free of charge within 12 hours of placing it. After that, production has begun and the order can no longer be cancelled.

Return Policy

Last updated: September 2026

No-Return Resolution

AnniTee products are made to order, so we handle problems without requiring you to return anything. If your item arrives damaged, defective, misprinted, or incorrect, simply email support@annitee.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo — we will ship a free replacement or refund you in full. Keep the original item, donate it, or recycle it.

Why We Don't Accept Returns

Each product is printed individually for your order rather than pulled from warehouse stock. A returned custom item cannot be restocked or resold, so instead of making you pay for return shipping and wait for processing, we resolve issues directly with a replacement or refund. It's faster for you and less wasteful for everyone.

Exchanges

Because items are made to order, we can't process traditional size exchanges. If we made an error (you received a size different from what you ordered), we'll replace it free. If you selected a size that doesn't fit, contact us anyway — we review these case by case and will do our best to find a fair solution.

How to Start a Claim

  1. Email support@annitee.com within 30 days of delivery.
  2. Include your order number and clear photos of the issue.
  3. Tell us your preference: free replacement or full refund.
  4. We respond within 24 hours with the resolution.
