Return Policy

Last updated: September 2026

No-Return Resolution

AnniTee products are made to order, so we handle problems without requiring you to return anything. If your item arrives damaged, defective, misprinted, or incorrect, simply email support@annitee.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo — we will ship a free replacement or refund you in full. Keep the original item, donate it, or recycle it.

Why We Don't Accept Returns

Each product is printed individually for your order rather than pulled from warehouse stock. A returned custom item cannot be restocked or resold, so instead of making you pay for return shipping and wait for processing, we resolve issues directly with a replacement or refund. It's faster for you and less wasteful for everyone.

Exchanges

Because items are made to order, we can't process traditional size exchanges. If we made an error (you received a size different from what you ordered), we'll replace it free. If you selected a size that doesn't fit, contact us anyway — we review these case by case and will do our best to find a fair solution.

How to Start a Claim

  1. Email support@annitee.com within 30 days of delivery.
  2. Include your order number and clear photos of the issue.
  3. Tell us your preference: free replacement or full refund.
  4. We respond within 24 hours with the resolution.
