Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Every AnniTee product is made to order — printed and crafted just for you after you place your order. Our items are produced at trusted fulfillment facilities in the United States and Vietnam, depending on the product type, and shipped directly to your door.

Processing Time

All orders require 2–4 business days of processing before shipment. During this time your item is printed, quality-checked, and packed. Orders placed on weekends or US holidays begin processing the next business day. During peak seasons (Black Friday, Christmas), processing may take 1–2 additional days.

Shipping Times

After processing, estimated delivery times are:

  • United States: 5–10 business days
  • Canada: 10–15 business days
  • United Kingdom: 10–15 business days
  • Germany: 10–15 business days
  • Australia: 10–15 business days
  • Rest of world: 10–20 business days

Total delivery time = processing time + shipping time. These are typical estimates, not guarantees — carriers can occasionally run late during holidays or severe weather.

Tracking

You will receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. Please allow 24–48 hours for tracking information to update after you receive it. Orders containing multiple items may ship in separate packages, each with its own tracking number, at no extra cost to you.

Customs & Import Fees (International Orders)

Orders shipped outside the United States may be subject to import duties or taxes determined by your local customs authority. These charges, if any, are the responsibility of the recipient.

Incorrect Address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we are happy to reship it — the buyer covers the reshipping cost. If you notice an address mistake, email us within 12 hours of ordering and we will correct it before the order ships.

Lost or Stuck Packages

If your tracking has not updated for 10+ business days or your order seems lost, contact us at support@annitee.com — we will investigate with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, send a free replacement or issue a full refund.

Questions? Email support@annitee.com — we reply within 24 hours, 7 days a week.
