Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AnniTee ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from annitee.com.

Information We Collect

  • Order information: name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number (optional), and details of the products you purchase — used to fulfill and deliver your order.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment providers. We never see or store your full card number, CVC, or banking credentials on our servers.
  • Device information: browser type, IP address, time zone, and cookies — used to keep the store working correctly and understand how visitors use the site.
  • Communications: emails you send to our support team, so we can assist you and keep a record of your requests.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process and deliver orders, send order confirmations and shipping updates, provide customer support, prevent fraud, improve our store, and — only if you opt in — send occasional marketing emails, which you can unsubscribe from at any time with one click.

Sharing Your Information

We share information only with service providers who need it to operate the store: our e-commerce platform, payment processors, print-on-demand fulfillment partners (who receive your name and shipping address to produce and deliver your order), shipping carriers, and analytics/advertising services (such as Google and Meta) in aggregated or pseudonymous form. We never sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences, and measure site performance and advertising effectiveness. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; the store's core functions may not work correctly without essential cookies.

Data Retention & Your Rights

We keep order records as required for tax, accounting, and legal purposes. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing support@annitee.com. If you are a resident of California (CCPA), the EU/UK (GDPR), or another region with privacy laws, you have the rights granted by those laws, including data access, portability, and erasure — email us and we will honor your request within the legally required time.

Minors

Our store is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes & Contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version is always posted on this page. Questions or requests: support@annitee.com · AnniTee, 5830 E 2ND ST, STE 7000, Casper, WY 82601, United States.
