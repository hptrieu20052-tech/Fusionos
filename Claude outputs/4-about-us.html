About Sorawix

Sorawix is a print-on-demand store for people who love designs that mean something — family matching shirts for the holidays, first-birthday outfits, bachelorette crews, beach trips with your favorite people, and gifts made personal with a name or a photo.

Every item is printed to order in the United States. Nothing sits in a warehouse: when you place an order, your design is printed on fresh blanks, quality-checked, and shipped straight to your door with tracking. It's better for quality, and it means every piece is made just for you.

What we care about

  • Designs worth keeping — original artwork created by our own design team, refreshed for every season.
  • Quality you can feel — soft, durable garments (including genuine Comfort Colors® blanks on many styles) and vivid, long-lasting prints.
  • Real support — a real person answers every email within 24 hours, Monday–Saturday. If anything is wrong with your order, we replace it or refund it. Simple as that.

Sorawix is a brand operated by SORAWIX LLC, registered in Wyoming, United States. Thank you for supporting our store!
