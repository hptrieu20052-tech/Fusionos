Shipping Policy

Every AnniTee product is made to order — printed just for you after you place your order. Because of this, please allow both a production time and a shipping time.

Production time

Orders are produced within 2–5 business days after the order is placed. During peak seasons (Halloween, Christmas), production may take 1–2 extra days.

Shipping time (United States)

Standard shipping takes 5–10 business days after production is complete. You will receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

Order tracking

Every order ships with a tracking number. If you haven't received tracking within 6 business days of ordering, contact us at [SUPPORT-EMAIL] and we'll check on it right away.

Wrong address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, contact us within 12 hours of placing your order — once an order enters production we may not be able to change the address.

Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking shows no movement for 7+ days or your package is marked delivered but hasn't arrived, email us and we'll work with the carrier to resolve it — including sending a replacement when the package is confirmed lost in transit.

Refund & Return Policy

Each AnniTee item is custom-printed to order, so we don't hold stock and can't resell returned items. That said, we stand behind our products 100% — if anything is wrong with your order, we'll make it right.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund — your choice. No need to ship anything back.

Size exchanges

Please check our size chart carefully before ordering. Because items are made to order, we can't accept returns for ordering the wrong size. If you're between sizes or unsure, email us before ordering and we'll help you choose.

Order cancellation

You can cancel or change your order within 12 hours of placing it. After that, your order enters production and can no longer be cancelled.

Personalized details

For personalized items, please double-check names, dates, and spelling before checkout. We print exactly what you enter, so we can't offer refunds for customer-entered mistakes — but contact us anyway and we'll do our best to help.

How to reach us

Email [SUPPORT-EMAIL] with your order number. We respond within 24 hours on business days, and every issue is resolved within 2–3 days.
