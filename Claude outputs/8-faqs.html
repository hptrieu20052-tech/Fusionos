Frequently Asked Questions

Ordering

How do I order a personalized item?
On the product page, choose your style, size, and color, then fill in the personalization fields (names, numbers, or text) shown above the Add to Cart button. What you type is exactly what we print — please double-check spelling before ordering.

Can I change or cancel my order?
Yes — free of charge within 12 hours of placing the order. Email support@annitee.com with your order number and the change you need. After 12 hours, production has started and changes are no longer possible.

What payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All payments are processed over encrypted, PCI-compliant connections — we never see or store your card details.

Shipping

How long until I receive my order?
Every item is made to order: production takes 2–4 business days, then shipping takes about 5–10 business days within the US (Canada, UK, Germany, Australia: 10–15; rest of world: 10–20). Total time = production + shipping.

How do I track my order?
You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships — or use our Order Tracking page anytime. Allow 24–48 hours for tracking to start updating.

My order shows multiple packages — is that normal?
Yes! Different product types are printed at different facilities, so items from one order may arrive in separate packages on different days, at no extra cost.

Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship worldwide. International orders may be subject to local customs duties or taxes, which are the recipient's responsibility.

Sizing & Products

How do I pick the right size?
Every product page includes a size chart photo — measure a shirt you already love and compare. Our tees run true to size; Comfort Colors styles are slightly roomier. Full details on our Product Details & Sizing page. Still unsure? Email us before ordering — we're happy to help.

How do I care for my printed apparel?
Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors. Tumble dry low or hang dry. No bleach, and don't iron directly on the print. Treated well, the print will stay vivid for years.

Issues & Refunds

My order arrived damaged / misprinted / wrong — what now?
We'll fix it fast: email support@annitee.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we'll send a free replacement or issue a 100% refund — your choice, no need to return anything. See our Refund Policy.

Do you accept returns?
Because every item is made to order, we resolve issues with replacements or refunds instead of returns — faster for you, less wasteful for everyone. Details in our Return Policy.

I ordered the wrong size — can you help?
Made-to-order items can't be exchanged like warehouse stock, but contact us anyway — we review every case individually and will always look for a fair solution.

Still have a question?

Email support@annitee.com — a real person replies within 24 hours, 7 days a week.
