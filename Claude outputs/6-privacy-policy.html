Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Sorawix (operated by SORAWIX LLC) collects when you use this website, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Order information: your name, email address, shipping address, phone number, and the items you purchase.
  • Personalization content: names, dates, or photos you submit to customize a product.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment providers — we never see or store your full card number.
  • Browsing data: cookies and similar technologies that help the site function (cart, login) and help us understand how the store is used.

2. How we use it

  • To produce and deliver your order — including sharing the necessary details (name, address, personalization content) with the printing facility that produces it and the carrier that delivers it.
  • To send order confirmations, tracking updates, and reply to your support requests.
  • To send marketing emails only if you've opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time with one click.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

3. What we never do

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the store (production, delivery, payment processing, email), and only the minimum they need.

4. Personalization photos

Photos you upload for personalized products are used solely to produce your order and are not used for any other purpose.

5. Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings; parts of the store (such as the cart) may stop working without them.

6. Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (except data we must keep for legal or accounting reasons). Email contact.sorawix@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.
