Care Instructions

To keep your print vivid for years: machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors · tumble dry low or hang dry · no bleach · do not iron directly on the print · no dry cleaning.

A note on colors

Product photos are mockups; actual print colors may vary slightly due to screen settings and the printing process — this is normal for printed apparel. Garment-dyed items (Comfort Colors) may also have slight shade variations between batches; that's part of their vintage charm.
