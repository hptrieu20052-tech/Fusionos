Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your satisfaction is our priority. Because every AnniTee item is printed on demand especially for you, our refund process is simple and doesn't require you to ship anything back.

Our 30-Day Guarantee

If your order arrives with any of the following issues, we will send a free replacement or issue a 100% refund — your choice, and no return needed:

  • Damaged in transit (torn, stained, broken)
  • Misprinted, blurry, or wrong design
  • Wrong item, wrong size, or wrong color compared to what you ordered
  • Significantly different from the product description or photos
  • Confirmed lost in transit by the carrier

To claim, email support@annitee.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue. We resolve most claims within 24 hours.

How Refunds Are Issued

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 3–5 business days of approval. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take an additional 5–10 business days for the amount to appear on your statement.

What Is Not Covered

  • Ordering the wrong size or changing your mind after the item has been produced — each piece is made uniquely to order, so please review the size chart on each product page carefully before purchasing. If you're unsure, email us first — we're happy to help you choose.
  • Damage caused by improper care (see care instructions on the product page).
  • Shipping delays by the carrier within the estimated windows in our Shipping Policy.

Even in these cases, reach out — we review every request individually and will always look for a fair solution.

Order Cancellation

You can cancel or edit your order free of charge within 12 hours of placing it. After that, production has begun and the order can no longer be cancelled.
