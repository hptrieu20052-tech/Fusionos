Shipping Policy

Every Sorawix product is made to order — printed just for you after your order is placed. Please allow both a processing time and a shipping time. All products are printed and shipped from our production facilities in the United States, and we ship worldwide.

Processing time

Orders are produced within 2–4 business days after the order is placed. During peak seasons (Halloween, Christmas), production may take 1–2 extra days.

Shipping time

Shipping time starts after production is complete:

  • United States: 5–10 business days
  • Canada: 10–15 business days
  • United Kingdom: 10–15 business days
  • Germany: 10–15 business days
  • Australia: 10–15 business days
  • Rest of world: 10–20 business days

Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding US public holidays. International orders may occasionally be delayed by customs processing, which is outside our control.

Order tracking

Every order ships with a tracking number, sent to your email as soon as the order leaves the printing facility. If you haven't received tracking within 5 business days of ordering, contact us at contact.sorawix@gmail.com and we'll check on it right away.

Customs, duties & taxes (international orders)

Orders shipped outside the United States may be subject to import duties or taxes charged by your country. These fees are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in the product price or shipping cost.

Wrong address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, contact us within 12 hours of placing your order — once an order enters production, the address may no longer be changeable. Orders returned to sender because of an incorrect customer-provided address can be reshipped at the customer's cost.

Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking shows no movement for more than 7 days (US) or 15 days (international), or your package is marked delivered but hasn't arrived, email us. We'll work with the carrier to locate it — and if the package is confirmed lost in transit, we'll send a free replacement or refund your order.
