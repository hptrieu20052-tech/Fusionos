Shipping Policy

Every Sorawix product is made to order — printed just for you after your order is placed. Please allow both a processing time and a shipping time. All products are printed and shipped from our production facilities in the United States, and we ship worldwide.

Processing time

Orders are produced within 2–4 business days after the order is placed. During peak seasons (Halloween, Christmas), production may take 1–2 extra days.

Shipping time

Shipping time starts after production is complete:

  • United States: 5–10 business days
  • Canada: 10–15 business days
  • United Kingdom: 10–15 business days
  • Germany: 10–15 business days
  • Australia: 10–15 business days
  • Rest of world: 10–20 business days

Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding US public holidays. International orders may occasionally be delayed by customs processing, which is outside our control.

Order tracking

Every order ships with a tracking number, sent to your email as soon as the order leaves the printing facility. If you haven't received tracking within 5 business days of ordering, contact us at contact.sorawix@gmail.com and we'll check on it right away.

Customs, duties & taxes (international orders)

Orders shipped outside the United States may be subject to import duties or taxes charged by your country. These fees are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in the product price or shipping cost.

Wrong address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, contact us within 12 hours of placing your order — once an order enters production, the address may no longer be changeable. Orders returned to sender because of an incorrect customer-provided address can be reshipped at the customer's cost.

Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking shows no movement for more than 7 days (US) or 15 days (international), or your package is marked delivered but hasn't arrived, email us. We'll work with the carrier to locate it — and if the package is confirmed lost in transit, we'll send a free replacement or refund your order.

Refund & Return Policy

Each Sorawix item is custom-printed to order, so we don't hold stock and can't resell returned items. That said, we stand behind every product 100% — if anything is wrong with your order, we'll make it right.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, printed on the wrong product, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery with:

  • your order number, and
  • a clear photo of the issue.

We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund — your choice. You don't need to ship anything back.

Size exchanges

Please check the size chart on each product page carefully before ordering. Because items are made to order, we can't accept returns or exchanges for choosing the wrong size. If you're between sizes or unsure, email us before ordering — we're happy to help you choose. As a general rule for a relaxed fit, size up.

Order cancellation & changes

You can cancel or change your order within 12 hours of placing it — just email us with your order number. After 12 hours, your order enters production and can no longer be cancelled or changed.

Personalized items

For personalized products, please double-check all names, dates, photos, and spelling before checkout. We print exactly what you enter, so we can't refund customer-entered mistakes — but contact us anyway and we'll do our best to find a solution.

Refund processing

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 3–5 business days. Depending on your bank, it may take a few additional days to appear on your statement.

About Sorawix

Sorawix is a print-on-demand store for people who love designs that mean something — family matching shirts for the holidays, first-birthday outfits, bachelorette crews, beach trips with your favorite people, and gifts made personal with a name or a photo.

Every item is printed to order in the United States. Nothing sits in a warehouse: when you place an order, your design is printed on fresh blanks, quality-checked, and shipped straight to your door with tracking. It's better for quality, and it means every piece is made just for you.
